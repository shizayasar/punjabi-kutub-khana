About Punjabi Kutub Khana

پنجابی کتب خانہ Punjabi Kutub Khana is a free digital library of Punjabi literature written in Shahmukhi. Walk through its halls, pick a book off the shelf, and read it in your browser.

Why it exists

Generations of Punjabi poetry, qisse and scholarship were printed in Shahmukhi, from the Sufi poets to the lithograph presses of old Lahore and Amritsar. Many of those books now survive in only a few libraries, or on crumbling paper in family collections. This library gathers them in one place, so anyone in Punjab or anywhere in the world can read them, for free.

How books are chosen

  • Every book is in Shahmukhi script.
  • Books are added only if they are in the public domain (in Pakistan, 50 years after the writer’s death), or shared with the permission of the writer, their family, or an open licence.
  • Each book shows where its scan came from and who edited or digitized it.
  • Scans are hosted on Internet Archive.

With thanks

To the Panjab Digital Library, Maktabah.org, Internet Archive, and the scholars and volunteers whose editions and scans make this library possible, including Dr. Faqir Muhammad Faqir.

Get in touch

Want to suggest a book, share a scan from your family’s collection, or report a problem with a book? Email contact@punjabikutubkhana.org. If you hold the rights to a book here and want it removed, write to us and it will be taken down promptly.
